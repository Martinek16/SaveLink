Privacy Policy
===============

SaveLink values your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

- All data (links, categories, groups, settings, backups) is stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local.
- The extension requests access to webpages to enable saving pages/links and utilities like link checking. No page contents are sent to any server.
- Network requests are limited to user-initiated operations (e.g., checking if a link is reachable) and fetching favicons from Google Favicon service over HTTPS.
- No analytics, trackers, or third-party SDKs are embedded.
